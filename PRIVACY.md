# Privacy Policy – The Development – Soccer

**Last updated: 13 November 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how The Development – Soccer (“the Game”), handles information when you play the Game or request support.

## 2. Information Collected During Gameplay

The Game operates offline and does not automatically collect or transmit personal information through gameplay.

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address or other personal information to play.

The Game does not include advertising networks, third-party analytics tools or tracking technologies. It does not sell personal information or track you across other apps or websites.

## 3. Local Game Data

Gameplay runs locally on your device. Match and tournament state are held on the device while the Game is running.

The Game does not upload gameplay data to developer-operated servers or provide a cloud-save service.

## 4. Support and GitHub

If you choose to request support through GitHub Issues, we can see the information you submit, including your GitHub username, issue description and any attachments. We use this information to respond to requests and investigate problems.

GitHub issues are public and may remain visible until edited or removed. Please do not include sensitive personal information. You can edit or delete your own issue content through GitHub, subject to GitHub’s controls.

GitHub separately processes information when you visit this repository or use its services. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The Game does not automatically collect personal information from children or other players. No account or personal information is required to play.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the Game’s features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions or support, please follow the contact instructions on our Support page and open an issue in this repository.

Please remember that GitHub issues are public.
