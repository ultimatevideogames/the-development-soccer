# The Development – Soccer
### Support

Welcome to Wrongwright Homes. The complaints have reached the football pitch.

Pick your squad, take on furious homeowners and unleash ridiculous special moves across five rounds of retro football chaos.

**Score. Insult. Repeat.**

## How to Play

1. Tap **PRESS START**.
2. Choose five characters. Your first selection becomes the goalkeeper.
3. Tap **TO THE PITCH!**, then **KICK OFF!**.
4. Touch and drag on the pitch to move your controlled player.
5. Use **PASS**, **SHOOT** and **INSULT** during the match.
6. Fill the **SUPER** meter through goals, successful tackles and insults. Use the arrow button to choose a special move, then tap its name when the meter is full.

Win matches to progress through the tournament and reach the final.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I play offline?

Yes. The game works offline and does not require an account.

### Are there advertisements or in-app purchases?

No. The game does not include advertisements, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

### Why can’t I start after choosing my players?

You must select exactly five players before continuing.

### Why can’t I use a special move?

Your SUPER meter must reach 100% before you can activate a special move during normal play.

### What happens if a match ends in a draw?

The match enters golden-goal extra time.

### Can I try again after losing?

Yes. Choose **REMATCH** to retry or **CHANGE SQUAD** to select a different team.

## Report a Problem

For technical support, questions or feedback, open the **Issues** tab at the top of this GitHub repository and select **New issue**.

Please include:

- Your device model.
- Your iOS version.
- The game version.
- A description of the problem.
- Steps to reproduce it.
- A screenshot, if useful.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal details.
